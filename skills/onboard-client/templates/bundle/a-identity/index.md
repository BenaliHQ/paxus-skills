# A. Identity & relationship

Durable facts about who this client is. Set once at onboarding; revised only
when the business itself changes.

* [Industry](industry.md) - two-to-three-sentence business description
* [Ownership](ownership.md) - who owns the business
* [Entity relationships](entity-relationships.md) - related entities and intercompany accounts
* [Contacts](contacts.md) - everyone we talk to, and the portal users
* [Working with this client](working-with-this-client.md) - communication preferences and quirks
