# Step 6: When the client replies or W-9s come back

Run when the person says the client sent emails, a W-9 arrived, or "update <client>'s 1099s".

- **Emails from the client**: add each to the tracker (Vendor email). Give the controller the
  vendor heads-up email to send before the Tax1099 requests: the client and lead on To,
  every vendor BCC'd, the Tax1099 sample screenshot attached inline:

  > Subject: <Client> Electronic W9 Request
  >
  > Hello,
  >
  > You will be receiving an email soon from Tax1099 Support requesting you to click a link to
  > complete a W9. Your email will tell you the company name that is requesting the W9 be
  > filled out. This email is safe and the link is secure and does expire after 30 days. This
  > allows us to gain an electronic copy of your W9 on behalf of <Client>. I've included a
  > screenshot of a sample email so you know what to be looking for. If you do not receive it
  > soon, please check your spam. Do not hesitate to reach out with any questions you have.

  And a one-line heads-up reply to the client first: "Thank you for these email addresses! We
  will be sending an email out to them to notify them they will be getting an electronic W-9
  request. It will look like it is just going to you; however, all of your vendors will be
  blind copied so they all get the notification without getting each other's information."
- **Bounced or auto-replied emails**: note the bounce in the tracker, ask the client for a
  new address.
- **A W-9 received**: the person saves it to `Taxes > W-9`. Read box 3a; update the
  evaluation file's L–N, P, R (date received) and NOTES; mark the tracker Received; list the
  QBO update (TIN, address, Track 1099) for the vendors being filed. A corporation with no
  exception: tracker "Not needed", file row NO.
- **The controller sends Tax1099 requests**: only then do the dates go in W-9 REQUESTED and
  the tracker's "W-9 requested in Tax1099", and Status becomes "Requested".
