# Welcome email template

Sent **TO** primary contact, **CC** Lead + Controller + Staff + lisa@paxuscpa.com, with the rendered welcome packet PDF attached.

## Placeholders the skill must fill

| Placeholder | Source |
|---|---|
| `{Client Name}` | FC display name (Phase 1 handoff) |
| `{Greeting}` | "Good morning" or "Good afternoon" — pick by send time |
| `{Contact First Name}` | First name of FC primary contact |
| `{Onboarding}` | Onboarding fee from Phase 1 handoff |
| `{Cleanup Deposit}` | 50% of cleanup floor / lower range |
| `{Cleanup Deposit Date}` | The 20th of the month **prior** to the first monthly draft (e.g., monthly draft Aug 5 → cleanup deposit July 20). Spelled-out month + day. |
| `{Monthly}` | Monthly accounting fee |
| `{Month}` | Spelled-out month monthly billing begins (e.g., "September") |
| `{Lead}` | Lead Accountant — first name only |
| `{Controller}` | Controller — first name only |

**Staff Accountant is intentionally "to be determined"** in the email even when assigned internally — the packet page 2 names them, but the client-facing email keeps that line open until services begin.

`Welcome to Paxus, {Client Name}!`

- Monthly accounting: ${Monthly}, auto-drafted on the 5th of each month starting {Month} 5.

Your team

- Lead Accountant & primary contact: {Lead}

- Staff Accountant: to be determined (we'll confirm before monthly services begin)

Please send anything sensitive through the client portal rather than email; that's also where your monthly financials will live. Reach out to me directly with any bank-access or portal questions — I'll be making sure your QBO file is connected and ready.

Looking forward to working with you,
