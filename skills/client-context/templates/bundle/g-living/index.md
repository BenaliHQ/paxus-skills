# G. Living context

Files that grow as work happens. Updated monthly or continuously.

* [Onboarding status](onboarding-status.md) - checklist pointer and outstanding items
* [Cleanup outcomes](cleanup-outcomes.md) - durable results and discovered coding rules
* [Known challenges](known-challenges.md) - what breaks repeatedly
* [Review exceptions](review-exceptions.md) - this period's findings; starts empty
* [Delivery history](delivery-history.md) - delivered periods and package links
* [Meeting calendar](meeting-calendar.md) - standing dates that move work timing
* [Escalation map](escalation-map.md) - who unblocks what when the client goes quiet
* [Meeting records](meeting-records.md) - summaries, decisions, client commitments
* [Correction & skill feedback log](correction-and-skill-feedback-log.md) - one-line corrections; the AI-readable source of truth
