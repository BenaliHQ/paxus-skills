# Budget vs. forecast

Budget: static, decided once a year; for nonprofits it cannot change without
board approval. Forecast: the living document, revised as actuals roll in
(dropped grants, additions), so the client can be proactive rather than
reactive. Forecasting applies to CFO clients only.
