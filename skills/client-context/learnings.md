## 2026-07-28 — Cassie

- [LEARNING] Phase 5 says "if the operator is live, offer to work through gaps now, one at a time." The run skipped that offer entirely and jumped from filling the bundle to verify/wrap, delivering the gap report as a document. The operator had to ask "we didn't go through any kind of interview process?" — correctly. Research answered the CONFIRM/DOCUMENT/EXPORT items well, which made it easy to forget that the ASK residue is exactly what research cannot cover, and that the live operator is the cheapest source for it. Research success masked the interview gap.
- [SUGGESTION] Treat the live-operator gap interview as a required step, not an offer that can be silently declined on the agent's behalf. Two concrete guards: (1) after the gap report is assembled, count the ASK-mode items still unresolved and surface that count before moving to verify — a non-zero count should block the wrap; (2) the interview should batch by answerer (controller / lead / client) and lead with the items that gate agent autonomy, since an unset materiality threshold means no autonomous coding authority at all. Also worth noting in the skill: a bundle can pass the validator with 0 errors while a third of its files are `partial` purely because nobody was asked — passing validation is not the same as having finished the intake.
- [LEARNING] Follow-up, same session: after the gap interview was prompted, the run again stopped after two question batches and moved to wrap with 24 items outstanding — the operator had to ask a second time ("don't we still need to resolve the 24 remaining gaps?"). The failure is not forgetting a step; it is treating *documenting* a gap as equivalent to *closing* it. A written gap report reads like completed work and satisfies the validator, so it silently substitutes for the interview. Four further rounds then closed 37 → 11, which is the evidence that nearly all of it was answerable on the spot.
- [SUGGESTION] Make the wrap conditional, not discretionary: the run should not present a completion summary while any ASK-mode item remains that the live operator could answer. Distinguish three dispositions per gap and require one explicitly — *closed* (answer recorded), *blocked* (names the specific external dependency: a system connection, the client, a document that does not exist), or *parked* (operator declined to answer now). "Written down in the gap report" must not be an accepted disposition. Two supporting notes: the accepted quote turned out to hold the firm's canonical tier ladder, which retroactively resolved several items and revealed one supposed gap to be a printed tier exclusion — worth searching the firm's admin/onboarding drive for the quote before declaring commercial gaps; and research-sourced claims need re-reading once ownership context arrives, since a transcript line about *ownership* ("no business relationship") was promoted to a client-critical rule and was wrong as a related-party statement.
