## 2026-08-26 — first run by a lead other than the author

- [LEARNING] The skill itself ran well and the agenda was the standout: the lead sent it to
  the client ahead of the call and most of the item-6 list came back answered before the call
  started. The call went faster and the client arrived knowing what was coming. Now noted in
  both the skill and the HOW-TO as worth doing — as a suggestion, not a required step.
- [LEARNING] Debrief created too many files. Updating the prep notes plus writing an outcomes
  layer, a recap, and backups left a folder the lead had to pick through, and gives
  `/client-context` several overlapping versions of the same facts to read. Fixed: two living
  documents updated in place, recap email as the only new file, no backups (Docs version
  history is the undo).
- [LEARNING] The agenda should not be left alone after the call. Ticked item-6 boxes and a
  covered / partly / not-reached mark per item make it the outstanding-items list the lead
  actually returns to. Previously the skill deliberately left it as the artifact of what was
  planned; that was the wrong call.
- [LEARNING] Information arrives between prep and the call on most engagements — firm admin
  fills in figures, the client sends documents, the call moves. Re-running prep from scratch
  duplicated work and files. Added an explicit refresh mode that updates in place.
- [LEARNING] Both the agenda and the recap were hand-edited before going out to sound less
  generated. Treated as a real requirement rather than a nice-to-have: plainer voice in the
  drafts, and an explicit prompt to the lead to add a line of their own.
- [LEARNING] Findings from the QuickBooks review landed after the call and belonged in the
  recap, but the transcript had no trace of them. Debrief now asks for anything learned
  outside the call before drafting.
- [SEQUENCE] Most of the confusion in this run was not the skill. The dossier hadn't been
  written when the lead started, so the file sitting in the folder was another client's; the
  lead filled in a dossier herself, then had to redo the prep once the real one arrived; and
  the Financial Cents kickoff project's task list predates the current process. Two fixes on
  our side: a dossier that is missing, stale, or another client's now stops the run and routes
  to firm admin, and the skill never writes a dossier. The Financial Cents task list is firm
  admin's to update — not something this skill should compensate for.
- [SUGGESTION] Open question outside this skill: whether the per-client cleanup/findings folder
  one lead has been using is a firm-wide pattern or a one-off. Worth settling before two skills
  start writing findings to different places on the same client.
