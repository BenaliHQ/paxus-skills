# Default Kickoff Call Agenda — the 11-item spine

The firm-standard run order for a new-client kickoff call. Every call sheet the
`/kickoff-prep` skill builds follows this spine. Items are **fixed**; the content
*inside* items 4 and 6 is customized per client from the dossier.

Defined by the firm's controller, 2026-07-29.

| # | Item | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Welcome** | Welcome everyone to the meeting. |
| 02 | **Client intro** | A quick intro from the client and any guests they bring. |
| 03 | **Meet your team of three** | Introduce the Paxus team and their roles. |
| 04 | **Timeline and cleanup** | Cover onboarding and the cleanup timeline, identify open items, set expectations. *Open items customized from the dossier.* |
| 05 | **Communication** | Client portal for sensitive data, confirm who is authorized, set up two-factor authentication. |
| 06 | **Gathering information** | Request the credentials and documents still open — loans, assets, EIN, prior return, anything else missing. *Checklist customized from the dossier's open items.* |
| 07 | **Monthly workflow** | Explain the monthly accounting workflow and communication frequency. |
| 08 | **Reporting preferences** | Ask their comfort level reading financials, and whether they prefer numbers or charts. |
| 09 | **Tools and training** | Do they need Dext, and does a training need scheduling? |
| 10 | **Open floor** | Ask whether they have any questions. |
| 11 | **90-day call** | Explain what the 90-day call is. Either schedule it live, or set a reminder to schedule it. Either way it carries into the recap email — as a confirmed date or as an item to schedule. |

## Notes on the spine

- **Billing has no standalone item.** Onboarding fee, cleanup deposit and
  floor/ceiling, monthly amount and first draft date are carried as reference
  under item 04, where the Lead is already setting expectations. Operator
  decision, 2026-07-29.
- **Items 04 and 06 are the only customized ones.** Everything else is the same
  script every time. This is what makes the sheet buildable from the dossier
  without invention.
- **Item 11 branches.** Scheduled → capture the date and carry it into the
  recap. Not scheduled → an action item to schedule, also carried into the recap.
- **No-cleanup clients** need an item-04 variant — the header reads as an error
  when there is no cleanup in scope.
